# Genre Mechanism Research（题材机制研究）

Investigate a genre claim without collapsing market facts, premise structure, audience psychology, and plot into one explanation.

## Context pointers（上下文指针）

Read only what the current branch needs:

- For the overall research method, evidence labels, Semantic Neighborhood Search（语义邻域检索）, literature saturation, and experiment stopping rules, read `../../research/02_研究执行协议_证据推理与实验停点.md`.
- For market-demand layers, read `../../research/01_短剧市场需求科学研究框架.md`.
- For Premise Architecture（故事前提架构） and the current research boundary, read `../../research/00_短剧题材研究_层级地图.md`.
- If the topic already has a folder under `../../research/genre-studies/`, read its latest relevant files before creating a parallel explanation.

## Process（执行流程）

### ◆ Frame（定界）

Rewrite the question as one researchable phenomenon and identify the active layer: market phenomenon, Premise（故事前提）, Audience-side Value（观众侧价值）, Population Need State（群体需求状态）, or the connection between two layers.

**Completion criterion:** one active layer is explicit; adjacent-layer questions are parked rather than silently mixed in.

### ◆ Reality Check（现实检查）

Verify that the phenomenon to be explained actually exists. Distinguish label frequency, supply volume, ranking, playback, revenue, and story structure instead of treating them as interchangeable.

**Completion criterion:** every load-bearing market or work-structure premise is either supported or explicitly marked unknown.

### ◆ Competing Hypotheses（竞争假设）

For causal or motivational questions, establish at least two live explanations before searching for confirming evidence.

**Completion criterion:** the phenomenon is not bundled with a single preferred explanation.

### ◆ Research（研究）

Apply the research protocol in `../../research/02_研究执行协议_证据推理与实验停点.md`. Search the user's wording plus the relevant semantic neighborhood and prefer high-trust primary research, systematic reviews, Meta-analysis（元分析）, direct experiments, and strong bridge literature.

**Completion criterion:** the protocol's search gate is satisfied, or the status is explicitly `[文献检索未完成]`.

### ◆ Evidence Map（证据映射）

Label each major conclusion using the evidence vocabulary defined in the research protocol. Do not promote evidence for a general psychological mechanism into direct evidence about short-drama audiences.

**Completion criterion:** each major conclusion's wording matches its evidence status.

### ◆ Stop or Continue（决定停点）

Choose one:

- continue the current mechanism layer;
- mark `[机制层阶段性暂停：非文献穷尽]` when the next layer has higher information value;
- mark `[实验待执行]` only after the protocol's literature-saturation gate is passed and competing explanations still cannot be distinguished from existing evidence.

### ◆ Persist（沉淀）

Write substantial new findings into the existing topic chain under `../../research/genre-studies/<题材>/`. Update the global hierarchy map only when the hierarchy itself changes.

**Completion criterion:** there is one authoritative research trail, not a second parallel version of the same conclusion.

## Output（输出）

Report only:

- Current Node（当前节点）
- What is established
- Competing explanations
- Evidence status
- Current stopping point
- Knowledge asset created or updated
- Highest-value next research branch
