# Judge

Converge on the strongest justified claim without erasing uncertainty.

## Process

### 1. Normalize the claims

Separate genuinely different claims from wording differences. Preserve incompatible claims as separate candidates.

**Completion criterion:** the comparison is between claims at the same scope and level of specificity.

### 2. Weigh evidence

For each claim, assess:
- source fitness;
- methodological quality;
- evidence independence;
- directness;
- scope match;
- counterevidence;
- alternative explanations.

Do not count expert roles or source quantity as votes.

**Completion criterion:** the reason one claim is stronger than another is evidence-based and explicit.

### 3. Assign epistemic status

Use:
- **High confidence（高可信）**
- **Moderate confidence（中等可信）**
- **Tentative（暂定）**
- **Working hypothesis（工作假设）**
- **Unknown（未知）**

State the scope of the status.

**Completion criterion:** every material conclusion has a confidence state and a stated boundary.

### 4. Preserve disagreement

If evidence does not resolve a conflict, keep it unresolved and state what evidence would change the judgment.

## Output

Return:
- strongest justified conclusion;
- evidence basis;
- strongest counterevidence;
- scope / boundary;
- epistemic status;
- unresolved questions;
- what would change the judgment.

## Boundary

Judge is evidence adjudication, not majority voting and not rhetorical synthesis.
